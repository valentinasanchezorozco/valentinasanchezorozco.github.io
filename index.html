Area 3 · Coaching

Instructional Coaching & Teacher Development

I have more than two years of experience as an instructional coach across several roles and organizations. How I give feedback: I follow a strengths-first coaching cycle. I observe the class (live or recorded), name what is working, agree on one or two growth areas, and co-design an action plan that we follow up in the next cycle.

About me

From Antioquia to York, and back to Colombian classrooms

I believe English opens doors, and teachers are the ones who hold them open.

I was born and raised in Antioquia and have lived in many corners of Colombia since. Quindío holds a special place in my heart. I'm a first-generation Latina from a low-income family, the youngest of three sisters, and a proud aunt to two nephews and a niece. At home there's also Brownie, the dog who keeps me grounded.

I trained as a foreign language teacher at Universidad de Córdoba, where I co-created the English program's first radio show and found my passion for research. Today I go back as a guest lecturer in the MA in English Teaching and I mentor master's students as they finish their theses.

Scholarships changed my path. With the U.S. Department of State's Community College Initiative I studied early childhood education in Boston, graduating with a 4.0 GPA. With a Chevening Scholarship I completed an MA in Applied Linguistics for Language Teaching at the University of York, with Distinction. In York I also worked as an English tutor and team leader at York College.

Back in Colombia, I coordinate programs where policy meets the classroom. I have led the academic side of Barranquilla's bilingual strategy with the British Council and taught rural communities in the Amazon. I now lead curriculum and instruction at Generation Colombia.

I also founded EmpowerThem, a grassroots foundation that opens educational doors for young people and women, because I know what one opportunity can do for a family like mine.

My nonprofit · Founder & community leader since 2024

EmpowerThem Foundation

Empower one, empower all. EmpowerThem works in Montería, Córdoba, to expand access to education for children, young people and women through English, mentoring and professional development, in line with UN SDGs 4 and 5.

I lead it as a leader for my community, not as a CEO: side by side with volunteers, families and young people, deciding together what the neighborhood needs.

Mission

Improve the lives of women, children and vulnerable people in Montería through holistic education, gender empowerment, sustainable practices and professional development.

Vision

A thriving community where every woman and child has the knowledge, skills and confidence to break the cycle of poverty.

Reach

100+ students in workshops on scholarships and global opportunities, plus community art-in-English workshops for children.

Community art-in-English workshop for children, Montería.

Chevening Mentoring Program

A volunteer program where I guide young Colombians applying to the Chevening Scholarship: group sessions, essay feedback from volunteer reviewers and up to three one-to-one mentoring sessions, with a focus on leadership and giving back.
